03 — FAQ

Before you apply

Is this remote or on-site? +
Strictly on-site at Juspay's office in Bengaluru. The whole point is a live room — researchers, triagers, and the target in one place for 24 hours. Selected hackers get the exact address after confirmation.
Does it cost anything to enter? +
No. Registration is free. Meals and workstations are covered on-site, and shortlisted researchers travelling to Bengaluru can request travel support during registration.
How are the 30 spots picked? +
Our security team reviews every application — bounty track record, disclosed work, and the surfaces you hunt. Handles with public, reproducible impact stand out. It's curated, not first-come.
How is the ₹3,50,000 split? +
Top three placements take fixed prizes, and a severity + first-blood pool rewards the highest-impact and earliest valid findings. Full rules of engagement and reward tables are shared with selected hackers.
What do I need to bring? +
Your laptop, your toolkit, and a government ID for entry. Power, network, and caffeine are on us. Test credentials and scope are handed out at kickoff.
